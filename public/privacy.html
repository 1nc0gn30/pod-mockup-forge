Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

1. Client-Side Confidentiality

POD Mockup Forge is architected for zero-leakage creator privacy. All image uploads, canvas rendering, color mapping, and aspect fit mathematical transforms are executed in-memory directly in your browser. We never store, inspect, or resell your artwork or listing assets.

2. Payments & Licensing

Purchases are securely processed by Stripe or DePay Solana smart contracts. We do not store credit card numbers or private crypto keys. Your license token is stored locally in your browser's localStorage for instant offline reactivation.

3. Telemetry & Analytics

We do not use invasive third-party ad trackers. Operational telemetry is restricted to local server uptime and anonymous aggregate rendering statistics.
